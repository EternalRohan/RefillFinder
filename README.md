# 💧 RefillFinder

A free Android app that helps students locate nearby drinking water coolers across multi-story campus buildings and track water availability per floor.

## ✨ Features
* **Nearest Cooler Finder:** Quickly locate drinking water refill points across campus blocks and floors.
* **Floor-by-Floor Status:** Update and view whether a water cooler on a specific floor is working or out of water.
* **Offline Access:** Fast, reliable local storage so campus block and cooler details work anytime without internet.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Kotlin** & **Jetpack Compose** (MVVM Architecture)
* **Room Database**
